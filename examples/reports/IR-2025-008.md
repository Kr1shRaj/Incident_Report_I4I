# Incident Report IR-2025-008

*Fictional example report for testing the pipeline.*

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Date | 30 April 2025 |
| Site | Warehouse A, high-bay racking, aisle 4 |
| Reported by | Marcus Webb |

## Description

A carton fell from the top level of the racking in aisle 4 when a pallet was pulled out
by reach truck RT-05. It landed next to David Kim, who was not hit. No one was injured.

## Cause

The pallet had been overloaded and stock was not shrink-wrapped, so a carton slid off.

## Recommendations

- Shrink-wrap all stock stored above 3 metres.
- Install anti-collapse mesh on high-bay racking.
