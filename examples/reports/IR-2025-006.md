# Incident Report IR-2025-006

*Fictional example report for testing the pipeline.*

- **Date:** 2 April 2025
- **Location:** Warehouse A, battery charging room
- **Reported by:** Marcus Webb

## What happened

While changing a forklift battery, Sam Patel splashed battery acid on their forearm.
They were not wearing the required face shield and gauntlets. The eyewash and shower
station was used immediately.

## Injury

Minor chemical burn to the forearm, treated at the local clinic. Sam returned to work
the next day, so 1 day was lost.

## Cause

Required PPE was not worn while handling battery acid.

## Recommendations

- Keep PPE at the charging station and check it is worn.
- Retrain charging-room staff on acid handling.
