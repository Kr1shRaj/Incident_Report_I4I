# IR-2025-003 — Manual handling injury

*Fictional example report for testing the pipeline.*

**Date:** 21 February 2025
**Location:** Warehouse B, returns area
**Reported by:** Priya Nair

## Summary

Jordan Lee strained their lower back while lifting a 32 kg carton of returned
appliances from the floor onto a workbench without help. No lifting aid was available
in the returns area.

## Outcome

Jordan saw a physiotherapist and was given light duties. They lost 4 working days.

## Cause

Heavy carton lifted alone from floor level; no lifting aid in the returns area.

## Recommendations

- Provide a lift table in the returns area.
- Two-person lift rule for items over 25 kg.
