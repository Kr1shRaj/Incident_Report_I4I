INCIDENT REPORT
Fictional example report for testing the pipeline.

Report number: IR-2025-007
Date of incident: 2025-04-17
Location: Warehouse B, mezzanine
Reported by: Priya Nair

What happened
A box cutter blade broke while Elena Ruiz was opening shrink-wrapped stock on the
mezzanine. A fragment cut her left hand. A colleague, Ben Carter, applied a dressing.
She needed three stitches at hospital and returned to work the following week after
3 days off.

Root cause
Worn, non-retractable box cutters in use; safety cutters had run out.

Actions recommended
1. Replace all box cutters with self-retracting safety cutters.
2. Set a minimum stock level for safety cutters.
