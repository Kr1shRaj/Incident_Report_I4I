INCIDENT REPORT
Fictional example report for testing the pipeline.

Report number: IR-2025-002
Date of incident: 2025-02-03
Location: Warehouse A, aisle 12
Reported by: Marcus Webb (Health and Safety Officer)

What happened
Liam Okafor slipped on spilled hydraulic oil in aisle 12 and fell, bruising his hip and
elbow. The oil had leaked from reach truck RT-02 overnight and had not been cleaned up.
First aid was given on site with an ice pack. Liam returned to work the same shift.

Root cause
A leaking hydraulic hose on reach truck RT-02 and no start-of-shift floor inspection.

Actions recommended
1. Replace the hydraulic hose on RT-02 and check the other reach trucks.
2. Add a floor inspection to the start-of-shift checklist.
3. Place spill kits at the end of every aisle.
