INCIDENT REPORT  (fictional example report for testing the pipeline)

Ref: IR-2025-004
Date: 9 March 2025
Place: Yard, gate 2

During a delivery, a lorry driven by an agency driver clipped the gate barrier while
turning. The barrier arm snapped. Nobody was hurt. Gate 2 was closed for two hours
while the arm was replaced.

Cause: The turning space at gate 2 is too narrow for articulated lorries.

Recommendation: Direct articulated lorries to gate 1 until gate 2 is widened.
