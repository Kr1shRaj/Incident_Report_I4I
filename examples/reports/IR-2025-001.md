# Incident Report IR-2025-001

*Fictional example report for testing the pipeline.*

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Date | 14 January 2025 |
| Site | Warehouse B, loading dock 3 |
| Reported by | Priya Nair, Shift Supervisor |

## Description

At about 06:40 a forklift operator, Tom Reyes, reversed into a stack of pallets while
loading a trailer. A pallet fell and struck the lower leg of a picker, Ana Costa, who was
walking behind the forklift. The reversing alarm on the forklift (unit FL-07) was found
to be faulty.

## Injuries

Ana Costa suffered a fractured ankle and was taken to hospital. She was off work for
21 days. Tom Reyes was not injured.

## Cause

The reversing alarm on FL-07 had failed and pedestrians were walking in the forklift lane.

## Recommendations

- Repair and test all forklift reversing alarms weekly.
- Mark separate pedestrian walkways at loading docks.
- Refresher training on forklift lane rules for all pickers.
